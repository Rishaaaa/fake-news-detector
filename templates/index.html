How it works

  1. Your text is cleaned: URLs, HTML, punctuation and stopwords removed.
  2. Remaining words are reduced to their base form (lemmatization).
  3. TF-IDF converts the text into a numeric feature vector.
  4. A trained classifier scores that vector and returns a probability.
